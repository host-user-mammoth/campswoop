    Which campgrounds can it watch?

    Campgrounds on Recreation.gov (US national parks, national forests and Army Corps lakes), Parks Canada, BC Parks, Ontario Parks and Washington State Parks, 4,767 in all, plus private campgrounds that book through Firefly Reservations: paste the booking link. The map also shows other campsites from OpenStreetMap to help you find their booking page, but we can't watch those.

    How fast do I need to book?

    Fast. Open sites usually go within minutes, so every alert links straight to the booking page.

    Can I watch for a festival or a big game?

    Yes. Watch a campground near it for the event dates. Camping passes sold as event tickets can't be watched. Going with friends? After you sign up, share the dates and they get the form filled in.

    How do I stop alerts?

    Every email has a link to stop that alert and a link to see all of yours.

    Is my email shared?

    No. It's used only to send your alerts.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. You book and pay the campground directly.
